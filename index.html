What we automate

We take on any business process that runs on manual work. These six come up most often, and every build is shaped around your process, not a template.

Scheduling and dispatch

Assign the right person to the right job without the whiteboard. Skills, certifications, availability and customer rules are checked automatically, and changes reach the field in one step.

Schedules that hold up through a sick day or a vacation.

Billing, invoicing and reconciliation

Invoices go out when the work is done, not when someone finds time. Payments are matched to invoices automatically, and the paperwork holding up payment is flagged before it ages.

Faster billing and fewer write offs.

Compliance and document tracking

Every certificate, license and required document is tracked against its expiration date. Reminders go out before anything lapses, and the record is ready when an auditor or lender asks.

Nothing expires without warning.

Inventory and purchasing

Stock levels, reorder points and purchase approvals live in one place. Shortages surface before an order stalls, and every purchase goes to the right approver.

Fewer stockouts and no surprise spending.

Credentialing and records management

Staff, provider and vendor files stay complete and current, with renewals tracked automatically. A lapsed credential never stops billing or holds up a job.

Complete records without the chasing.

AI data analysis and process insights

We analyze your own transaction, schedule and billing data to find where work stalls, where errors repeat and where money leaks. You get a clear list of fixes, ranked by what each one is worth.

Decisions based on your data, not guesses.

Every solution connects to the accounting, operations and document systems you already use, so nobody retypes anything.

Common questions

Do we have to replace the systems we already use?

No. Most projects connect to your current accounting and operations tools and replace the spreadsheets and manual steps between them.

How soon will we see something working?

You see a working prototype of your own process within days of the first conversation, running against realistic data rather than described in a document. The finished software follows in weeks.

How is a project priced?

The build is one fixed price, agreed in writing before building starts. After launch, a flat monthly care plan covers hosting, support and small changes. Anything bigger is priced in writing before the work begins.

Who owns the software?

You do. The code written for you, your data and the documentation are yours, and if you ever move on you take all of it with you. Shared building blocks we reuse across projects come with a permanent license to use them.

Your care plan starts. We host the software, watch it, keep it secure and backed up, fix what breaks, and make small changes as your process shifts. What the plan includes is agreed in writing with the project, so you know before you commit.
