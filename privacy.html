Privacy.

This site collects almost nothing. Here is exactly what it does collect and why.

What this site collects

Only what you type into the process review form: the description of your process, who runs it today, the systems it touches, your company, your name and your email address. Nothing else on this site asks you for information.

Why we collect it

To reply to you and to prepare for the call. That is the only reason. We do not add you to a mailing list, and we do not send anything you did not ask for.

Who else sees it

The form is delivered by our hosting provider, which processes the submission so it reaches us. Beyond that, nobody. We do not sell, rent or share what you send, and we do not pass it to any other company.

Tracking and cookies

This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics and carries no advertising or tracking scripts. The typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts, which means Google receives a request for the font files when the page loads. Nothing you type is involved in that request.

How long we keep it

We keep what you send for as long as we are in contact about the work, and after that only as long as our records require. Ask us to delete it and we will.

Your choices

You can ask what we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted. Email us and we will do it.

How to reach us

Email info@waymarklab.com with anything about this page or about the information you have sent us.
